# Disclosure Rules

## When to disclose
Include a disclosure block whenever the **subject of the post — or a company named in it — is a 1789 Capital holding.** Otherwise, omit it and write neutrally/factually.

## Language (use verbatim)
- Single holding:
  > The author is a Limited Partner in 1789 Capital, which holds a position in **[Company]**.
- Multiple holdings named:
  > The author is a Limited Partner in 1789 Capital, which holds positions in **[A], [B], and [C]** — several of the names referenced in this post.
- Always append:
  > Personal commentary and editorial analysis. Not investment advice, not an offer or solicitation.

## Vulcan Elements (special — direct stake)
> Disclosure: The author is an early-stage investor in **Vulcan Elements** and a Limited Partner in 1789 Capital, which also holds a position.

## Caption rules (Instagram)
- Firm name stays **anonymous** — never "Koch Capital" in a caption.
- Sign-off: **"— Frontier-first."**
- Use the **"The author is…"** formulation for all disclosures (never "Koch Capital is…").
- The standing disclaimer always sits **above the hashtags.**

## Edge cases (how recent posts were handled)
- A post about a facility/product **operated by** a holding discloses that holding (e.g., **Stargate** → Crusoe operates Abilene; **Colossus** → xAI operates it).
- **SPCX** discloses SpaceX **and** xAI (the S-1 folds in the xAI merger).
- **Polymarket launch** discloses Polymarket plus the named holdings traded (SpaceX, Databricks, Anduril).
- **Claude** (Anthropic) carries **no** disclosure and is written factually, not promotionally.

> When unsure whether a company is held, check the DISCLOSE list above. If it's not on the list, don't disclose.
