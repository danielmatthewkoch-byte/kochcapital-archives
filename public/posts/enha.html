Enhanced.

Enhanced Group Inc. — the world’s first sports league built on performance enhancement — debuts on the NYSE under ENHA. A $1.2B listing via SPAC merger, with the inaugural Enhanced Games weeks away.

By KOCH. VC. May 8, 2026

The Debut.

Enhanced Group Inc. began trading on the New York Stock Exchange under ENHA on May 8, the final step of a SPAC combination (APAD) at a roughly $1.2B valuation and up to $200M in proceeds. The redemption deadline passed April 29, shareholders cleared the merger May 1, and APAD delisted May 7. The company pairs two engines: the Enhanced Games — an annual Las Vegas competition with a $1M bounty for breaking the 50m freestyle world record — and a direct-to-consumer performance-medicine platform spanning testosterone protocols, recovery, and longevity. The inaugural Games land May 24 at Resorts World Las Vegas with a $25M athlete compensation pool across swimming, track, and weightlifting; Hunter Armstrong, Fred Kerley, and Ben Proud are among those signed for $250K+ purses. The bet, in founder Aron D’Souza’s framing: scientific transparency and medical oversight beat banned-substance enforcement — and audiences will pay to see what humans can actually do.
