Robinhood.

Robinhood just opened its platform to autonomous AI agents. The line from advisory to agentic — the one Plaid and Ramp were inching toward — got crossed today.

By KOCH. VC. May 27, 2026

The Lead.

Once an AI agent can execute, the brokerage stops being an app and starts being an API. Robinhood is the first major U.S. broker to formally cross the line.

"
Democratize finance
— to AI agents."
VLAD TENEVCO-FOUNDER & CEO · ROBINHOOD · On the agentic trading launch · May 27, 2026

"Our mission has always been to democratize finance for all, and now, that mission extends to AI agents. Whether you're executing a specific trading strategy, or simply buying the cheapest flight available, you can build agents to help manage your investments while spending safely and autonomously." — VLAD TENEV, ROBINHOOD.
