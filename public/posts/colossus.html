Colossus.

Musk's Memphis megacluster scaled to 2 gigawatts and 555,000 GPUs. But the real story is power. Compute is no longer the bottleneck — electricity is.

By KOCH. VC. May 20, 2026

The Lead.

The AI race is now an energy race. Colossus — xAI's Memphis facility — represents roughly $18B in hardware, but the binding constraint has shifted to gigawatt-scale power. An estimated $1.4T is needed for AI data-center electrification by 2030, drawing GE Vernova, nuclear, and the full power stack into the AI trade. Whoever solves power first wins.
