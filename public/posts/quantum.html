Frontier Tech · Washington Goes All-In · 05.22.26

Quantum.

Washington is awarding $2 billion to nine quantum firms — and taking equity stakes in return. The most aggressive U.S. bet yet on winning the quantum race with China. PsiQuantum among the recipients.

By KOCH. VC. May 22, 2026

The Lead.

A new industrial-policy playbook: the government becomes a shareholder, not just a grant-maker. The $2B package, routed through NIST under the CHIPS Act, funds nine firms in exchange for equity, and quantum stocks soared on the news. PsiQuantum — silicon-photonic qubits, a $7B September-2025 valuation (up from $3.2B in 2021), with sites in Brisbane and Chicago — is among the named recipients.
