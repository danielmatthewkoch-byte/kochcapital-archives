Physical AI.

At Davos 2026, WEF declared the foundational era of robotics over. At CES, Jensen Huang called it "the ChatGPT moment for physical AI." The third layer of the AI stack is here.

By KOCH. VC. May 11, 2026

The Stack.

The AI conversation now has three distinct layers, each at a different stage of deployment:

  • Generative AI = answers. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. Cloud-based. Single-turn responses to questions.
  • Agentic AI = actions. Salesforce Agentforce, custom agents. Software that reasons, plans, and executes multi-step tasks autonomously.
  • Physical AI = embodiment. Humanoids, autonomous vehicles, industrial robots. Hardware that perceives and acts in the real world.

Rule to Reason.

The WEF/BCG framework identifies three tiers within physical AI, mapped to historical phases:

  • Rule-based (1970s onwards) — Deterministic. Every motion repetitive and predictable. Factory floor robots.
  • Training-based (2015 onwards) — Reinforcement learning. Handles variability. Most warehouse robots today.
  • Context-based (now) — Multimodal transformers + vision + language. Understands the why, not just the how. Unstructured environments unlocked.

The Numbers.

$5 trillion humanoid robot market projection by 2050 (Morgan Stanley). 58% of enterprises already deploying physical AI to some extent (Deloitte survey of 3,200 leaders); 80% projected within 2 years. 1,000× compute acceleration for robotics over the past 8 years per BCG — the technical inflection's root cause. Asia-Pacific leading globally; manufacturing, logistics, defense leading by sector.
