By KOCH. VC. May 14, 2026

The Numbers.

Cerebras Systems opened at $350 — nearly double the $185 IPO price. The stock hit an intraday high of $386 before being halted briefly for volatility. It closed at $311.07, up 68% from the offer price. Market cap at close: approximately $95 billion. Fully diluted valuation: approximately $86 billion. Trading volume was extraordinary as institutional and retail investors competed for shares from a book that was 20× oversubscribed at pricing.

The Context.

This was the largest U.S. tech IPO since Uber's 2019 listing and the biggest pure-play AI hardware debut in market history. It also validated the entire AI hardware IPO pipeline that was waiting on this signal — SpaceX (June), OpenAI (Q3-Q4), Anthropic (October). CEO Andrew Feldman's stake at close: approximately $1.9 billion. Co-founder and CTO Sean Lie: approximately $1 billion. Up to $6.38B in total proceeds if underwriters exercise the full overallotment option.
