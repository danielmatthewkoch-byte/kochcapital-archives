Semiconductors Power AI · 05.15.26

Chips.

The semiconductor industry hits $975B in 2026. AI chips alone: $500B. Doubling to $1.5T by 2030. Five companies command the stack. Compute is the new oil. The whole AI economy runs on this foundation.

By KOCH. VC. May 15, 2026

The Stack.

Five layers, end-to-end: equipment (ASML for EUV lithography), foundries (TSMC, Samsung, Intel manufacture the chips), AI accelerators (NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Cerebras, Groq design them), memory (SK Hynix, Micron, Samsung feed them), and end users (every hyperscaler increasingly building their own custom silicon). Every layer is supply-constrained. Every layer is strategically critical.

The Foundries.

TSMC is the undisputed king — Q1 2026 saw $35B revenue, +58% net income YoY, with 75% of revenue now from advanced chips and 2nm capacity sold out for 2026. Samsung is the #2 with $1T+ market cap and a Texas fab building Tesla's AI6 chip through a $22.8B deal lasting until 2033. Intel is finally resurgent on the 14A process with the Terafab partnership announced in April 2026 and Arizona expansion underway.

The Gorilla.

NVIDIA commands approximately 92% of the AI training market with the H200 → Blackwell → Rubin roadmap. The CUDA software ecosystem locks in developers across every layer. Most strategically, NVIDIA secured over 50% of TSMC CoWoS packaging capacity through 2027 — Google was forced to cut its 2026 TPU production from 4 million to 3 million units because they couldn't get CoWoS allocation. That's Google. Cutting its own AI chip production because of NVIDIA's lock on packaging.

The Challengers.

AMD is the credible #2 with MI300 series shipping at scale and MI400 next-gen launching in 2026. UALink open standard challenges NVIDIA's NVLink lock-in. Broadcom is the silent giant of custom silicon — building Google's TPUs, Meta's MTIA, and a $10B custom AI accelerator deal with OpenAI announced September 2025. Plus the specialty plays: Cerebras (wafer-scale, just IPO'd at +68%) and Groq (inference specialist).

The Terafab.

Musk's mega-fab in Austin, Texas — partnership with Intel announced April 7, 2026. Intel's 14A process technology. SpaceX provides high-volume manufacturing expertise. Tesla and xAI provide primary demand. $25B initial investment. Target: 1 terawatt per year of compute production. Vertical integration: logic + memory + advanced packaging under one roof, bypassing the fragmented global supply chain. Musk personally toured Intel's Hillsboro fab on May 8.

Five layers. One stack. Every layer constrained. Every layer strategic.
