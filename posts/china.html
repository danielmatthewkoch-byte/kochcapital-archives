Trump-Xi Summit · Beijing · 05.14.26

China.

Trump landed in Beijing with Musk, Huang, Cook. First sitting U.S. president to visit China in roughly a decade. Combined CEO delegation net worth: about $1 trillion. Xi opened with the Thucydides Trap. AI chips. Rare earths. Taiwan. Iran.

By KOCH. VC. May 14, 2026

The Photo.

Musk, Huang, Cook, Rubio, Hegseth walking off Air Force One together in Beijing — the photo of the trip. First sitting U.S. president to visit China in roughly 10 years. The world's wealthiest individual and the world's most strategically important AI CEO, on Chinese soil, alongside the President. Huang was a surprise late addition — he joined the plane at an Alaska stopover after Trump's personal call. Tech & chips at the table: Musk, Huang, Cook, plus Mehrotra (Micron) and Amon (Qualcomm). Capital & finance: Fink (BlackRock), Schwarzman (Blackstone), Solomon (Goldman), Fraser (Citi), plus a JPM exec, Miebach (Mastercard), McInerney (Visa). Industrial: Ortberg (Boeing), Culp (GE Aerospace), Sikes (Cargill). Notably absent: Nadella, Pichai, Altman, Amodei, Bezos, Zuckerberg himself, GM, Disney. The omissions tell their own story — no frontier AI labs, no hyperscalers.

The Stakes.

Two-day summit agenda, May 14-15, four areas on the negotiation table plus tariffs and rare earths. AI chips: Nvidia's H200 sales to China currently restricted. Huang wants to unlock them. A modified H20 framework already negotiated. Frontier (Blackwell, Rubin) stays restricted. Rare earths: China restricted exports of 7 of 12 specific rare earth metals in April 2025, then announced 5 more would follow. Used in everything from EVs to fighter jets. Strait of Hormuz: per the White House, both sides agreed it must remain open. Xi expressed interest in buying more American oil to reduce strait dependency.

The Trade.

2026 vs 2017 — different game now. Xi has more leverage. U.S. holds: NVIDIA frontier chips, Cerebras wafer-scale, Micron HBM, Qualcomm mobile. China wants access to all four. Each tier has different export-control status. China holds: majority of global rare earth mining and processing. 7 of 12 already restricted, 5 more threatened. Needed for EVs, smartphones, fighter jets, satellites, missiles. Both hold dependency: Tesla Shanghai +26.7% Q1. 80% of US iPhones made in China. Apple, Tesla, supply chains, Boeing aircraft, soybeans. Decoupling is not a clean exit. Both sides know it. The chips-for-earths bargain is on the table.

Xi Opens.

Xi to Trump, opening remarks at the Great Hall of the People this morning: "Can the United States and China avoid the Thucydides Trap?" A direct reference to the historical pattern of war between rising and ruling powers — Xi posed this as his opening question. He then called Taiwan "the most important issue" in U.S.-China relations. If mishandled, he warned, it would push the bilateral relationship to a "dangerous" place. Closing: can the two countries meet major challenges together for global stability and work for "a brighter future" for humanity? Per CCTV state media broadcast.
