Data.

Big Four hyperscaler capex 2026: $725B — up 77% from $410B in 2025. Six layers of infrastructure. From silicon to servers to memory to networks to neoclouds to energy. Map of the physical AI economy.

By KOCH. VC. May 15, 2026

The Trajectory.

Big Four hyperscaler capex has grown 3.6× in two years: $200B (2024) → $410B (2025) → $725B (2026). Microsoft alone is spending $190B in FY26 — with $25B attributed to memory chip price inflation. Amazon: $200B. Alphabet: $175-185B. Meta: $115-135B. Oracle: $50B (+136% YoY). McKinsey projects worldwide AI capex of $6.7 trillion by 2030. The buildout has no clear ceiling — and Microsoft has an $80B Azure backlog that cannot be fulfilled due to power constraints.

The Stack.

Six tiers map the physical AI economy. Compute Silicon: NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Cerebras, Groq plus custom silicon (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, MSFT Maia, Tesla AI5/AI6). Servers + Solutions: Supermicro, Dell, HPE, Foxconn, Quanta, Wiwynn assembling the racks. Memory + Storage: SK Hynix, Micron, Samsung HBM consuming 30% of DC spend (4× over 2023). Network + Connectivity: Arista, Cisco, Coherent, Marvell, Lumen, Equinix providing GPU-to-GPU bandwidth.

The Neoclouds.

The new GPU-cloud tier sits between hyperscalers and the physical layer. Crusoe leads with $10B+ valuation and the Stargate flagship build. CoreWeave is public at $80B+ market cap. Nebius, Lambda, IREN follow. The DC operators — Equinix, Digital Realty, Iron Mountain, Vantage — provide the colocation backbone. Cooling and power gear: Vertiv (liquid cooling, mission-critical), Eaton (switchgear), Schneider Electric (DC infrastructure).

The Energy.

Power is THE constraint — not chips. Data center demand will hit 1,000 TWh globally in 2026, equivalent to Germany's entire electricity consumption. Constellation Energy secured a 20-year Three Mile Island restart deal with Microsoft. Talen Energy has the Amazon Pennsylvania nuclear PPA. Vistra is the largest competitive power producer. NextEra leads renewables. GE Vernova provides gas turbines, nuclear, and grid technology. Plus emerging plays: Form Energy (100-hour iron-air batteries), Redwood Materials (EV-battery microgrids), Crusoe (stranded gas at 1/13 standard rates).

The Future.

OpenAI planned $1.4T in data centers. Stargate has a $500B target with Crusoe building. Plus xAI Colossus, Anthropic-SpaceX (300 MW), Meta Louisiana 5 GW, Terafab 1 TW/yr. Project Suncatcher targets orbital data centers by 2027 (Google × SpaceX). And the cash cost: Amazon FCF turning negative, Alphabet FCF projected -90% in 2026, Meta FCF down up to 90%, Microsoft -28%. The buildout is consuming hyperscaler cash flows.
