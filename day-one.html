By KOCH. VC. May 14, 2026

The Price.

Cerebras priced its IPO last night at $185.00 — $25 above the already-raised range of $150-$160. $5.55 billion raised. $39.82 billion market cap at pricing. The 9-day trajectory: $120 midpoint → $155 midpoint → $185 final. A 54% move from original midpoint to final pricing in nine days. The original range ($115-$125, May 4) priced 28M shares for a $3.5B raise at $26.6B implied valuation. The amended range (May 11) lifted to $150-$160 with the float expanded to 30M shares and a 20×+ oversubscribed book per Reuters. Final pricing came in $25 above that already-revised range. Institutional demand priced through the ceiling.

The Journey.

From filing to first trade — 35 days of editorial coverage across four posts. 04.10 · Cerebras. First archive post. Cerebras files S-1. The 2024 IPO attempt was killed by G42 concentration. Take two: $510M revenue, 47% net margin, OpenAI deal lined up. 05.02 · The Book. Coverage of the oversubscribed book. Original $115-$125 range starts attracting institutional demand. Book grows rapidly through May. 05.12 · Peak. Range raised to $150-$160. 20× oversubscribed. The frenzy is the story. Burry's bubble call lands the same day. Both can be true. Both might be. 05.14 · Listed. Trading live.

The Backers.

Individual backers: OpenAI co-founders Altman, Brockman, and Sutskever — the founding trio personally on the cap table. Sam Altman appeared in the Cerebras roadshow personally. Customers: $20B+ multi-year deal with OpenAI announced January 2026 — 750 MW deployment plus future hardware co-design, plus a $1B working capital loan from OpenAI to Cerebras. Underwriters: Morgan Stanley led the book. Citigroup, Barclays, UBS as joint leads. Co-managers: Needham, Craig-Hallum, Mizuho, TD Cowen. Wall Street's full force. Strategic: March 2026 binding term sheet for AWS to deploy Cerebras systems at hyperscale — the first hyperscaler partnership, validating the inference scale-out thesis.

The Business.

FY 2025 revenue: $510M, up 76% YoY from $290M in 2024. The growth is real. 2026 projected revenue: roughly $1B at current pace, putting the IPO at a 51× trailing multiple. Net margin: 47% — profitable AI hardware, not a unicorn cash-burning story. Contracted revenue backlog at year-end 2025 per the S-1: $24.6 billion. Approximately 48× current annual revenue. The OpenAI 750 MW deal alone underpins multi-year visibility. The tech: CS-3 supercomputer — the world's largest commercial chip. Wafer-scale, specifically designed for inference scale-out — the workload eating an increasing share of all AI compute.
