Databricks.

The profitable outlier of the 2026 AI IPO wave. $134B valuation. $5.4B revenue run-rate. +65% YoY growth. Positive free cash flow. 4th most valuable private company on Earth. S-1 expected this summer.

By KOCH. VC. May 17, 2026

The Numbers.

Annualized revenue run-rate: $5.4 billion as of Q4 2025, up from $4.8B in mid-2025. Growth accelerated from 55% to 65% year-over-year. AI products alone generated $1.4B run-rate (approximately 26% of total revenue). Net retention rate sustained above 140%. Customer count: 20,000+ organizations, including 60%+ of the Fortune 500 and 800+ enterprises spending more than $1M annually. Free cash flow has been positive over the trailing twelve months — the only company in the AI IPO pipeline with this profile. Total funding raised: $7B+ in equity and debt.

The Outlier.

Of approximately 12 major AI IPO candidates, Databricks stands alone as the only profitable name. OpenAI is targeting $852B-$1T at IPO but burning cash with operating losses persisting. Anthropic is raising $30B at a $900B valuation per FT (May 2026) while still operating at substantial losses. SpaceX is targeting $1.75T but disclosure is limited. Cerebras IPO'd on May 14 at +68% but ran an adjusted operating loss in 2025. Databricks is the conservative position in the AI IPO frenzy — real revenue, real growth, real margins.

Vs Snowflake.

The disruption narrative is now clear. Revenue: Databricks $5.4B annualized vs Snowflake $4.7B FY26 — Databricks crossed Snowflake in 2025 and continues to widen the gap. Growth: +65% vs +29% — Databricks growing 2.2× faster on a larger revenue base. AI products: $1.4B at Databricks vs limited disclosure at Snowflake. Valuation: $134B private vs $58B public — 2.3× the multiple. If Databricks IPOs anywhere near its current private mark, expect significant downward pressure on Snowflake shares.

The Path.

Public market trajectory for 2026. S-1 filing expected mid-summer. Lead underwriters confirmed: Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley. Listing window: late 2026 to early 2027. Sits between SpaceX (June) and OpenAI/Anthropic (Q4) in the calendar. Nasdaq Private Market secondary trading marks shares at $200.82. The cap table includes Insight Partners, Fidelity, J.P. Morgan Asset Management, Andreessen Horowitz, BlackRock, Blackstone, Coatue, GIC, MGX, NEA, Ontario Teachers, T. Rowe Price, Temasek, Thrive Capital, 1789 Capital, and Qatar Investment Authority.
